About JNCT Rewa

Central India's Gateway to Engineering Excellence

Admission Process

Three Steps to Your JNCT Seat

1

Apply Online

Fill the online application with personal & academic details, upload marksheets and pay the ₹1,200 processing fee. Application ID is generated instantly.

2

Merit & Counselling

Shortlisting via MP-DTE / JEE Main merit or JNCT entrance aptitude test. Counselling rounds invite you to campus for document review and branch preference lock-in.

3

Verification & Fee Submission

Original document verification, medical fitness check and fee submission. Seat confirmed with provisional admission letter issued on the spot.

Eligibility — B.Tech: 10+2 with PCM ≥ 45% (40% reserved) · M.Tech: B.E./B.Tech in relevant branch · MBA: Graduation ≥ 50% with CMAT/XAT/MAT · Diploma: 10th Pass ≥ 50%. Lateral entry (10+2+3 Diploma holders) directly to 2nd year B.Tech.
